Account Deletion Request

Effective Date: September 1, 2026

At AORS, we respect your privacy and provide you with full control over your account and associated data. If you wish to delete your AORS account and all corresponding data from our relay infrastructure, you can submit a deletion request through the form below or directly via email.

What Happens When You Delete Your Account

  • Account Removal: Your account identifier, profile information, and authentication records will be permanently deleted from our active databases.
  • Device Unlinking: All paired devices will be immediately unlinked and logged out.
  • Ephemeral Data Purge: Any remaining temporary queue or relay metadata will be purged.
  • Retention Exceptions: Limited non-content records may be retained temporarily where required for security, legal compliance, or fraud prevention as described in our Privacy Policy.
