AORS Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 1, 2026 | Last Updated: September 1, 2026

AORS ("AORS," "we," "us," or "our") operates a secure, ephemeral OTP relay service (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, process, protect, retain, and delete information when you use the AORS Service.

AORS is designed around the principle of data minimization. Our Service is intended to relay authentication codes and related messages between authorized devices while minimizing the amount of information retained by our infrastructure.

We do not sell your personal information or OTP/message content to third parties. We do not use OTP message content for advertising, profiling, or training artificial intelligence models.

This Privacy Policy is intended to describe our practices in a manner consistent with applicable privacy and data-protection requirements, including, where applicable, India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act"), applicable rules and regulations issued under it, and applicable U.S. federal and state privacy laws.

1. Scope of This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to information processed in connection with: The AORS application and related software; AORS accounts; The AORS OTP relay functionality; Devices connected to an AORS account; Communications between authorized devices using AORS; Our websites, support services, and other services that link to this Privacy Policy.

This Privacy Policy does not necessarily apply to third-party websites, applications, operating systems, telecommunications providers, authentication providers, or other services that may interact with AORS. Those third parties maintain their own privacy policies and terms, and you should review their policies separately.

2. Our Privacy Principles

AORS is designed around the following privacy principles: Data Minimization, Ephemeral Processing, Automatic Deletion, No Sale of Personal Information, Security, and Limited Purpose.

3. Information We Collect

The information we process depends on how you use AORS. 3.1 Account Information: When you create or maintain an AORS account, we may process information necessary to establish and operate the account. Depending on the Service configuration, this may include: Account identifier; Username or similar identifier; Email address, where provided; Authentication credentials or authentication-related information; Device identifiers; Device registration information; Session information; Account creation and deletion information; Security and authentication events. We attempt to avoid collecting unnecessary personal information. Where an account can be operated without providing optional information, we will identify optional information where reasonably practicable.

4. OTP and Message Content

The primary purpose of AORS is to relay OTPs and related authentication messages between authorized devices. When an OTP is transmitted through AORS: The message is received by the AORS relay infrastructure. The message is processed temporarily for routing and delivery. The message is transmitted to the intended recipient device. The relay copy is discarded after successful delivery or expiration of the relay session. The recipient application automatically deletes the received OTP after 10 minutes from receipt, subject to applicable technical limitations. AORS is designed so that OTP/message content is not retained as a permanent message history. We do not intentionally maintain a historical database of OTP message content for ordinary Service operation.

5. Encryption

AORS uses encryption intended to protect information while it is transmitted between participating components of the Service. OTP messages and other sensitive information are protected using encryption mechanisms appropriate to the Service's technical architecture. Where encryption is used, it is intended to reduce the risk of unauthorized interception or access while information is being transmitted. However, no Internet-connected system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Accordingly, while we take reasonable measures to protect information, we cannot guarantee absolute security. Users are responsible for protecting their own devices, account credentials, authentication methods, and access to their AORS account.

6. Ephemeral Message Processing

AORS is designed as an ephemeral relay, rather than a permanent messaging platform. Message content may temporarily exist in volatile memory or other transient processing environments as necessary to: Receive an OTP; Authenticate a relay request; Determine the intended destination; Deliver the OTP; Confirm delivery; Handle temporary failures; Protect the Service against abuse and unauthorized use. We do not intentionally write ordinary OTP/message content to persistent storage for the purpose of maintaining a message history. After the message has been successfully delivered, or when the relay session expires, the message content is automatically discarded from the applicable relay process. Technical, security, infrastructure, or legal requirements may in limited circumstances result in the processing of non-content metadata, as described elsewhere in this Policy.

7. Automatic Deletion on Recipient Devices

AORS is designed to provide an additional privacy safeguard through automatic deletion. OTP messages received on the recipient device are automatically deleted 10 minutes after they are received. This feature is intended to reduce the amount of sensitive authentication information remaining on the recipient device. Automatic deletion may be affected by circumstances outside AORS's control, including: Device operating-system restrictions; Application termination; Device failure; Offline operation; Backups; Screenshots; Screen recordings; Notification previews; Accessibility features; Device-management software; Third-party software; User-created copies; System-level logs or diagnostics. Accordingly, automatic deletion should not be interpreted as a guarantee that every possible copy of an OTP has been removed from every system associated with a device. Users should not intentionally copy, screenshot, forward, or otherwise preserve OTPs unless necessary.

8. Routing Data and Metadata

To operate the relay service, AORS may process limited routing information. Routing information may include information such as: Source device or account identifier; Destination device or account identifier; Relay/session identifier; Message delivery status; Timestamp associated with relay activity; Connection information; Technical information required to maintain a secure connection; Authentication and security information. This information is used primarily to: Route OTPs to the intended recipient; Authenticate authorized devices; Maintain Service functionality; Detect and prevent unauthorized access; Protect the Service against abuse; Troubleshoot technical problems; Maintain security and reliability. We do not use routing information for behavioral advertising or profiling unrelated to the operation of the Service.

9. Information We Do Not Intend to Collect

AORS is designed not to require unnecessary collection of information. Unless specifically necessary for a particular feature, we do not intend to collect or maintain: Permanent OTP message histories; Permanent copies of OTP content; Advertising profiles; Detailed behavioral profiles; Message histories for marketing purposes; Sensitive message content for AI training; Personal information for sale to data brokers. The exact information processed may vary depending on the Service configuration and technical requirements.

10. How We Use Information

We may use information processed through AORS for the following purposes: Providing the Service (To authenticate users and devices and relay OTPs to authorized destinations); Security (To detect, prevent, investigate, and respond to unauthorized access, fraud, abuse, attacks, and other security incidents); Service Maintenance (To maintain, troubleshoot, diagnose, and improve the reliability and functionality of AORS); Account Management (To create, authenticate, maintain, and delete user accounts); Customer Support (To respond to requests, troubleshoot issues, and communicate with users when necessary); Legal Compliance (To comply with applicable laws, regulations, lawful governmental requests, court orders, or other legal obligations); Protection of Rights (To establish, exercise, or defend legal rights and protect AORS, our users, and other persons from harm or unlawful activity). Recipient Device: OTP messages are automatically deleted from the recipient device 10 minutes after receipt, subject to the limitations described in this Policy. Account Information: Account information may be retained while an account remains active or for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Service. Security and Operational Records: Certain limited metadata may need to be retained for security, fraud prevention, auditing, troubleshooting, legal compliance, or enforcement purposes.
